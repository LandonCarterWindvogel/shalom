About

Made here, for schools and clubs here.

Shalom Designs supplies school uniform items and embroiders for schools, sports clubs and community organisations in the Garden Route. The work is done in our workshop, on our own machines.

In the workshop, in Plettenberg Bay

The work

Cut, sewn and embroidered here.

School uniform items are made and finished in our workshop. Where we don't manufacture an item, we supply it and apply the badge, name or embroidery ourselves, so the finish is the one we control.

Embroidery runs on our own machine — school badges, club crests, names on items, and one-off work. That is why embroidery is quoted per job: the artwork, the placement and the quantity all change the price.

See our services

The detail

Finishing is the work.

Every garment we produce carries a Shalom Designs size label. Badges are placed and stitched to a consistent position, and names are applied so they stay readable after a full school year of washing.

We would rather confirm a price with you than publish a number we are not certain of. That is why some items on this site show a range and some show a single price — and why embroidery is quoted rather than listed.

How we price

Three rules, applied everywhere.

Exact prices are exact

A loose badge, a winter beanie and a name on an item at Bahia Formosa School each have one fixed price. No hidden add-ons.

Ranges are shown as ranges

Where an item costs more in a larger size, we show the lowest price and say "price varies by size" rather than pretend one price fits every size.

No price is invented

If we have not confirmed what something costs, we say "contact us for a quote" rather than publish a number.
